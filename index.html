Milestone

Perjalanan Kami

Four decades of innovation, growth, and commitment to delivering reliable electrical solutions across Indonesia. Empat dekade inovasi, pertumbuhan, dan komitmen dalam menghadirkan solusi kelistrikan yang andal di seluruh Indonesia.

1985

Begin with retrofit & Recondition Bermula dari Retrofit & Rekondisi

Started as a specialist in switchgear retrofit and panel upgrades, extending the life and safety of existing installations. Mengawali langkah sebagai spesialis retrofit switchgear dan pembaruan panel untuk memperpanjang masa pakai serta meningkatkan keamanan instalasi yang ada.

2005

R&D Focus Berfokus ke R&D

Expanded into research & development for MV/LV power panels (PHBTM, PHBTR) and insulators, aligning with PLN & IEC standards.

Memperluas fokus ke riset dan pengembangan panel listrik tegangan menengah maupun rendah (PHBTM, PHBTR) serta isolator yang selaras dengan standar PLN dan IEC.

2010

First PHBTM Production

Panel PHBTM Diproduksi Pertama Kali

Rolled out the first PHBTM panels, marking our transition from retrofit-only to full in-house manufacturing.

Meluncurkan panel PHBTM perdana yang menandai transformasi kami dari spesialis retrofit menjadi manufaktur penuh secara mandiri.

2018

First PHBTR Production

Launched PHBTR panels with improved protection, reliability, and easier maintenance for industrial and utility applications.

Panel PHBTR Diproduksi Pertama Kali

Meluncurkan panel PHBTR dengan sistem proteksi dan keandalan yang ditingkatkan, serta perawatan yang lebih mudah untuk kebutuhan industri maupun utilitas.

Frequently Asked QuestionPertanyaan Umum

Temukan informasi mengenai produk, kebutuhan proyek, proses penawaran, dan layanan purnajual kami. Find answers about our products, project requirements, quotation process, and after-sales support.

1 What products does EGA provide? EGA menyediakan produk apa?

EGA offers medium-voltage switchgear, distribution panels, compact substations, CT/VT, and protection and metering solutions.

EGA menyediakan switchgear tegangan menengah, panel distribusi, compact substation, CT/VT, serta solusi proteksi dan metering.

2 Can EGA customize products for specific projects? Apakah bisa membuat produk kustom sesuai kebutuhan project?

Yes. Configurations are customizable according to the single-line diagram, technical specifications, protection system, metering, interlocking, and site conditions.

Bisa. Konfigurasi dapat disesuaikan dengan single-line diagram, spesifikasi teknis, sistem proteksi, metering, interlocking, dan kondisi lokasi.

3 What standards are used for EGA products? Standar apa yang digunakan pada produk EGA?

Produk dirancang dan diuji berdasarkan standar yang berlaku sesuai jenis produk dan kebutuhan proyek, termasuk standar IEC serta persyaratan pelanggan.

Our products are designed and tested to meet applicable standards for each product type and project, including IEC standards and client requirements.

1 What information is required to request a quotation? Informasi apa saja yang diperlukan untuk meminta penawaran harga?

Pelanggan dapat mengirimkan single-line diagram, spesifikasi teknis, jumlah panel, rating sistem, kebutuhan proteksi, dan jadwal proyek.

To get started, customers can share their single-line diagram, technical specifications, required panel quantity, system ratings, protection needs, and project timeline.

2 How long is the production lead time? Berapa lama waktu tunggu produksi (lead time)?

Lead time varies depending on product type, configuration, components, panel quantity, and drawing approval.
Final schedules are confirmed in our quotation.

Waktu produksi bergantung pada jenis produk, konfigurasi, komponen, jumlah panel, serta persetujuan drawing.
Jadwal akan dikonfirmasi dalam penawaran.

3 Does EGA provide technical consultation? Apakah EGA menyediakan layanan konsultasi teknis?

Ya. Tim EGA dapat membantu melakukan kajian spesifikasi, pemilihan konfigurasi, proteksi, metering, dan kebutuhan teknis lainnya.

Yes. Our team assists with specification reviews, configuration selection, protection, metering, and other technical needs.

1 Does EGA provide installation and commissioning support? Apakah EGA melayani dukungan instalasi dan uji komisioning?

Yes.
EGA offers installation supervision, testing, commissioning, and energization according to the project scope.

Ya.
EGA menyediakan pendampingan instalasi, testing, commissioning, dan energize sesuai ruang lingkup proyek.

2 Are FAT and SAT available? Apakah tersedia layanan FAT dan SAT?

Yes. FAT can be conducted prior to delivery, while SAT is performed on-site in accordance with project agreements and requirements.

Ya. FAT dapat dilaksanakan sebelum pengiriman, sedangkan SAT dilakukan di lokasi sesuai kesepakatan dan kebutuhan proyek.

3 Does EGA provide maintenance and troubleshooting services? Apakah EGA melayani perawatan dan penanganan gangguan (troubleshooting)?

Yes.
Our services cover preventive maintenance, inspection, testing, troubleshooting, retrofitting, and component replacement.

Ya.
Dukungan mencakup preventive maintenance, inspeksi, pengujian, troubleshooting, retrofit, serta penggantian komponen.
