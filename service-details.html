Solusi Distribusi Tenaga yang Andal Reliable Power Distribution Solutions

Solusi switchgear kami dikonfigurasi berdasarkan single-line diagram proyek, filosofi operasi, kebutuhan proteksi, sistem metering, tegangan kontrol, dan kondisi lokasi. Our switchgear solutions are configured around the project single-line diagram, operating philosophy, protection requirements, metering system, control voltage, and site conditions.

EGA mendukung pelanggan mulai dari kajian teknis dan engineering panel hingga manufaktur, routine test, pengiriman, pendampingan instalasi, commissioning, pemeliharaan, dan troubleshooting. EGA supports customers from technical review and panel engineering through manufacturing, routine testing, delivery, installation assistance, commissioning, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

Kategori Produk Product Categories

AIS — Type-Tested

Switchgear insulasi udara terstandar untuk proyek yang membutuhkan performa terverifikasi dan kompartementasi yang jelas. Standardized air-insulated switchgear for projects requiring verified performance and clear compartmentalization.

AIS — Non-Type-Tested

Konfigurasi insulasi udara yang fleksibel untuk retrofit, penggantian, dan kebutuhan proyek non-standar. Flexible air-insulated configurations for retrofit, replacement, and non-standard project requirements.

Insulasi Penuh — Type-Tested Fully Insulated — Type-Tested

Switchgear ringkas dan terlindungi untuk instalasi yang membutuhkan efisiensi ruang dan pengurangan paparan lingkungan. Compact and protected switchgear for installations requiring efficient use of space and reduced environmental exposure.

Insulasi Penuh — Non-Type-Tested Fully Insulated — Non-Type-Tested

Konfigurasi ringkas khusus proyek yang mengintegrasikan susunan feeder, proteksi, metering, dan kontrol. Project-specific compact configurations integrating feeder arrangements, protection, metering, and control.
