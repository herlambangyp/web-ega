Milestone

1985

Begin with retrofit & Recondition

Started as a specialist in switchgear retrofit and panel upgrades, extending the life and safety of existing installations.

2005

R&D Focus

Expanded into research & development for MV/LV power panels (PHBTM, PHBTR) and insulators, aligning with PLN & IEC standards.

2010

First PHBTM Production

Rolled out the first PHBTM panels, marking our transition from retrofit-only to full in-house manufacturing.

2018

First PHBTR Production

Launched PHBTR panels with improved protection, reliability, and easier maintenance for industrial and utility applications.

Today

50,000+ Units Nationwide

Our products have reached 50,000+ installations across Indonesia—powering plants, utilities, and critical infrastructure with proven reliability.

Our growth in numbers

Over the years, EGA Tekelindo Prima has grown from a small retrofit workshop into one of Indonesia’s most trusted manufacturers of medium-voltage power systems. Each number reflects our dedication to engineering excellence, reliability, and customer satisfaction.
